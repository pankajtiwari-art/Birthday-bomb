For Jiya

Jiya,

Shayad tumhe kabhi realise nahi hua hoga, par tumhari simple si presence mere liye bahut important rahi hai.

Ek time tha jab main kisi cheez se zyada umeed nahi rakhta tha. Tumse baat karke lagta tha ki din thoda halka ho gaya. Tumne kabhi kuch solve nahi kiya — tum bas wahan thi, aur wahi kaafi tha.

Aaj tumhara din hai, toh main bas itna chahta hoon ki tumhe bhi zindagi mein utni hi warmth mile, jitni tum unknowingly dusron ki life mein le aati ho.

Happy Birthday, Jiya.

Stay exactly the way you are.

— Pankaj ❤️
